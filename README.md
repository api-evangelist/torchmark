Globe Life Inc. (NYSE: GL), formerly known as Torchmark Corporation, is an S&P 500 financial services holding company headquartered in McKinney, Texas. Founded in 1900, the company provides life insurance, supplemental health insurance, and annuity products through direct-to-consumer and agent distribution channels across the United States. Revenue in 2024 was $5.78 billion with total assets of $29.1 billion.
